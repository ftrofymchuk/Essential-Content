Plan your visit

Opening hours

We are open every Saturday and Sunday from 10:00 until 16:00.

Last entry is at 15:00.

Admission

  • Adults: £8.00
  • Children: £5.00
  • Under threes: free
  • Family ticket: £23.00

Getting here

The farm is approximately fifteen minutes from the nearest railway station.

Free visitor parking is available beside the entrance.

Accessibility

The shop, café and main animal area have step-free access.

Please contact us if you would like to discuss individual access requirements.

Do you have a question?

Send us an enquiry before your visit and we will be happy to help.
